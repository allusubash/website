Group Guidelines & Safety Rules

  • No Dangerous Driving: Rash maneuvers, inner-group overtaking, and street-racing are strictly prohibited.
  • Cost Transparency: Individual participants take care of their own fuel charges, fastag/tolls, and restaurant food items.
  • Vehicle Fitness: Check your engine oil, coolant levels, tyre pressure, and confirm you carry a working spare wheel before arriving.
  • Active Checklist: Valid personal identity cards, vehicle registration documents, active third-party insurance, and PUC certs are mandatory.
💡 100% Free: There is zero cost to register or participate with our collective group convoy network.
